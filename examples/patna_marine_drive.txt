NARRATION: The smell of roasted litti leads him to one of his favorite stalls.

NARRATION: He orders a plate of litti chokha, freshly served with chokha, chutney, and onions.

VISUAL: Aman takes a bite of litti chokha and enjoys it. Cut between his genuine reaction, the food, and the vendor continuing to cook. Natural facial expressions, realistic chewing and hand movement, cinematic close-ups, no exaggerated acting.

SCENE 6
VISUAL: Aman walks along the promenade holding a paper cup of chai. He pauses by the riverside railing, watches the Ganga, and smiles as Patna's lights shimmer behind him. Slow cinematic pullback, gentle evening breeze, realistic riverfront ambience.

SCENE 7
VISUAL: Nighttime closing shot of Patna Marine Drive, with the illuminated promenade, moving traffic, food stalls, and the Ganga in the background. Aman walks away through the crowd as the camera rises into a wide view of the riverfront. Photorealistic cinematic ending, smooth camera movement.
